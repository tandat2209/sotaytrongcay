# Review notes

Used for young-vine establishment, selecting a vigorous shoot for a straight trunk, keeping soil adequately moist without saturation, weed competition, and the dependence of irrigation on soil and growing conditions.

The source includes region-specific rates and fertilizer examples. Those values are not transferred into the pilot because the app does not yet know enough about local climate, soil, container, cultivar, or nutrient status.
