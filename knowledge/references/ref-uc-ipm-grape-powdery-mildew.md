# Review notes

Used to support that powdery-mildew prevention begins with early-season risk
review and that treatment timing depends on vine growth stage, infection risk,
weather, and the selected fungicide.

The source targets commercial California vineyards. Its products, rates, fixed
spray intervals, risk thresholds, and registrations are not transferred into
Vietnamese home-grower guidance. A local reviewer must confirm applicability,
and any pesticide use must follow the product label and local requirements.
