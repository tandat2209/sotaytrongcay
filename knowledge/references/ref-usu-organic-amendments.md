# Review notes

Used to identify compost, manure, and humic or fulvic products as distinct
organic-matter amendments and to bound claims about their effects on soil,
water, and nutrient retention.

The source cautions that humic-product effects may be small or masked by the
existing soil. The app therefore describes Humic only as optional soil support,
not as a guaranteed root treatment or replacement for fertilizer.
