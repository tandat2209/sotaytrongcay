# Review notes

Used for the need to support young vines, establish roots before relying on shoot growth, apply water to the root zone, avoid unnecessary wetting of foliage, and adapt watering to rainfall and growing conditions.

Regional volumes and seasonal timing are not transferred into the pilot as universal Vietnamese guidance.
