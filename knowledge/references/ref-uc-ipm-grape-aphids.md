# Review notes

Used only for general, review-stage aphid observation context: soft-bodied
sap-feeders may leave honeydew, and abundant feeding can distort young foliage.
The associated UC IPM home grape index lists aphids among grape pests but does
not establish a grape-specific Vietnamese treatment threshold.

This reference does not support diagnosis, virus attribution, products, doses,
or a spray schedule. A qualified local reviewer must establish any such claim.
