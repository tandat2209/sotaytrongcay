# Review notes

Vietnamese public technical article republished in 2006 from Báo Ninh Thuận
and attributed to research from the Cotton Research and Development Institute.
It supports the monitoring relevance of thrips from bud break through flowering
and fruit growth, sanitation and balanced care as preventive context, treatment
only after a threshold is reached, and stopping thrips spraying when berries
begin to ripen.

The material is old and does not define a current home-growing threshold,
registered product, dosage, protective equipment, or label instruction. It may
support review-stage monitoring and safety boundaries, but any current chemical
control claim requires qualified local review and current Vietnamese
registration and label evidence.
