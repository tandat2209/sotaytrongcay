# Review notes

Used for stage-specific nitrogen and potassium guidance, the N-to-K transition across the production cycle, nitrogen cessation before véraison, foliar nutrition timing, and the relationship between nutrient balance and berry quality.

Regional fertilizer rates and soil-test thresholds are not transferred as universal guidance because local soil, climate, and cultivar conditions vary.
