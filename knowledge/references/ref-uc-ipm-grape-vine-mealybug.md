# Review notes

Used to support review-stage observation of vine mealybug's waxy appearance,
its occurrence on grapevine parts including clusters, honeydew and sooty-mold
context, and the value of checking ant activity and collecting a sample when
identification is uncertain.

The source serves commercial California vineyards. It does not establish a
Vietnamese home-growing treatment threshold, pesticide selection, dose, or
schedule. Its treatment tables and region-specific timing are excluded.
