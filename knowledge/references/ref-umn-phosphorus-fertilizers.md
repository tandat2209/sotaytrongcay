# Review notes

Used to support the role of phosphorus in plant energy transfer and root growth and the requirement that phosphorus decisions depend on crop, soil properties, environmental conditions, nutrient analysis, and soil testing. The source also identifies diammonium phosphate (DAP) as a fertilizer containing nitrogen and phosphorus.

The source is not grape-specific. It does not justify recommending DAP by default or transferring a dosage, placement, or schedule into the app.
