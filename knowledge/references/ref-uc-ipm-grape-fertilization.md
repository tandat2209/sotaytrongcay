# Review notes

Used to support the need for adequate but non-excessive grape nutrition and the conditional use of potassium sulfate when potassium deficiency is established.

The source's regional rates are not transferred into the app. Fertilizer-group guidance does not establish deficiency, dosage, timing, or fertilizer eligibility by itself.
