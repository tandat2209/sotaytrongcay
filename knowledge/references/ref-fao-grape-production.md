# Review notes

Used for calcium and boron roles in grape pollination and fruit set, potassium and sugar accumulation during ripening, and tropical viticulture adaptations including forced dormancy and multiple production cycles per year.

FAO production volumes and regional spray schedules are not transferred as universal home-grower guidance.
