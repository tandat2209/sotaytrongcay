# Review notes

Used to support grape-specific context for nitrogen, potassium, magnesium, and
boron and the requirement to confirm suspected deficiencies with plant and soil
evidence rather than a single visible sign.

The source warns that poor fruit set has causes other than boron deficiency and
that excess boron can be phytotoxic. No regional rate or deficiency threshold
is transferred into the app.
