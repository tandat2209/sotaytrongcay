# Review notes

Used to verify generic, brand-free fertilizer sources including compost and manure, calcium nitrate, potassium nitrate, potassium sulfate, magnesium sulfate, and dolomitic lime.

Product analysis varies. Lime and dolomite remain conditional soil amendments rather than routine fertilizer recommendations, and no regional rate is transferred into the app.
