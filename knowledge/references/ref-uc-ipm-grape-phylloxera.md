# Review notes

Used to support review-stage context that grape phylloxera is a tiny root-feeding
insect, possible root swelling and vine decline require closer inspection, and
rootstock choice is a planting-level decision rather than a quick spraying task.

The source is not a diagnosis protocol for Vietnamese home growers. It does not
justify an in-app product, dose, rootstock recommendation, or treatment claim;
those require qualified local advice.
