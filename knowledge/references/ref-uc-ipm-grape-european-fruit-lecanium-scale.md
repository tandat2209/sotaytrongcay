# Review notes

Used to support review-stage observation of brown domed scale insects on woody
grape parts, honeydew and sooty-mold context, and the need to monitor rather
than infer a need to spray from one observation.

The source serves commercial California vineyards. Its calendar timing,
threshold interpretation, products, rates, and regional restrictions are not
transferred into Vietnamese home-growing guidance.
