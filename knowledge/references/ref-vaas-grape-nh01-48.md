# Review notes

Used to confirm that powdery mildew is among the diseases documented for the
Vietnamese grape cultivar NH01-48 and to support integrated prevention through
field sanitation, appropriate canopy density, balanced nutrition, suitable
watering, removal of crop residue, and the Vietnamese “4 đúng” principle when
chemical control is used.

This cultivar profile does not establish a universal bud-break treatment,
product, dosage, or repeat interval, and it is not generalized to every grape
cultivar. Those decisions remain subject to qualified local review and the
label and registration applicable where the plant is grown.
